fitlake

fitness data lake app

fitlake puts all my training data in one place: gym workouts, runs, MMA sessions, and sleep and heart rate from my watch.

From this data it builds an athlete model based on known methods from sports science:

  • readiness: a score every morning from heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and breathing
  • session load: Banister TRIMP from heart rate, and Foster session RPE
  • weekly load: Foster monotony and strain, and the last 7 days against the last 28
  • strength: estimated one-rep max for each lift (Epley and Brzycki formulas) and how fast it grows
  • muscle balance: hard sets for each muscle group per week
  • running: VO2max, easy and hard runs split by heart rate (Seiler), heart rate recovery

I built it when I started doing gym, running and MMA at the same time. I needed to balance the load, see my progress and avoid overtraining.

fitlake is private. It reads health data from Google Health, and Google allows this for other people only after a long security review of the app. So I have not decided yet how to share it.
