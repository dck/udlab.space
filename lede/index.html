lede

news and RSS summarizer

lede.udlab.space.

lede collects articles from Hacker News, Reddit, Lobsters, RSS feeds and email newsletters into one inbox.

Every article gets a one-line teaser. Open it to get a full summary and the community verdict: pros, cons and where people stand on the topic.

There is too much to read today, and it is easy to get overwhelmed. With lede I stay informed with one inbox a day.
