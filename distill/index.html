distill

book and article summarizer

github.com/dck/distill

distill makes books and long articles shorter but keeps their structure, main ideas and the author’s voice. The result reads like the same book, just denser.

It was my first attempt to deal with information overload. To pick the best algorithm for squeezing books and other long texts, I ran an experiment with an LLM as a judge.
